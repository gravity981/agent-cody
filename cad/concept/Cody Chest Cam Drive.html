Cody Chest Cam Drive

Chest cam drive for Cody's arms

Everything that moves the arms sits inside the chest. The motor lies across the upper chest, just in front of and below the shoulder axle, and carries two small eccentric discs, half a turn apart. Each arm joins the torso like an action figure: its sleeve cap turns around a hidden steel axle, and inside the chest it continues as a short lever pointing forward. The arm's weight rests the lever tip on its disc. As the disc turns, it lifts and lowers the tip, and the hand bobs on the keys. Drawn to scale for a bust about 105 mm high (5 px = 1 mm); sizes are first guesses for stage 4.

Sizes that make everything fit

The bust is scaled to the drive. With these sizes the whole drive fits in the upper chest with 2 mm walls, and the assembly stays within FIG-02 (≤ 150 mm, target 120–130 mm with table and platform).
