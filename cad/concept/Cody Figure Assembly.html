01 / ASSEMBLE

Inside first, character last.

  1. Assemble shoulder modules, motor and cam on the frame.
  2. Attach the frame to the platform and route the wiring.
  3. Fit the removable chest cover and push the head onto the neck.
  4. Add tabletop and notebook, connecting the LED wiring.
02 / SERVICE

Make the inside reachable.

Remove the chest cover to reach the redesigned front-access fasteners, motor and arm joints. The base must give independent electronics and USB access. The illustration does not prove that tools, parts or wires have a clear path.

03 / STILL TO PROVE

Concept, not fabrication.

Front fasteners, backrest, tabletop cutout, joint retention, fits, wire routing, print orientations, motion clearance and stability remain open. A neck-joint coupon is a candidate first physical fit experiment after the required design and print-review gates.

Approved direction: architecture section 8.4. Stage 4's prototype remains separate from this look-ahead.
